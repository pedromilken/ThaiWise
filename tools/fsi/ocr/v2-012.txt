b) The verb phaa 'to escort, to take! normally has sentence
complements as indicated in the following:
(1) khaw  phaa...

(3) raw duu talaat néam
khadw  phaa raw paj duu-—s talaat naam
'He escorted us to (go see) the Floating Market.!
phaa is used when referring to human beings. When objects
are referred to aw 'to take,! is used. When the direction
of the action is away from the speaker paj is used with phaa
or aw; when it is toward the speaker, maa.

phaa... paj/maa when referring to human beings.
ec) When the Verb Phrase maa naan léew is added to a sentence,
