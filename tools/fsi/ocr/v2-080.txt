THAI BASIC COURSE
25.1 VOCABULARY NOTES

a) pen araj (paj) 'What!'s the matter (with you)?! is the
normal way to inquire about what pain or ailment someone
has.

1. If one has some kind of disease, he may respond as
follows:
phim pen wat 'I have a cold.!
phdm pen khaj 'I have a fever.!
phdm pen kha jwat 'I have the flu.!
Or he may respond as follows:
phdm pen rok bit 'I have dysentery.!
phim pen r6ok s@jtan I have appendicitis.!
phdm pen rGok ahiwaa 'I have cholera.!
2. If one has an ache or pain, he will give its location:
phdm puat hia (Lit. I ache in the head.)
'I have a headache.!
phdm puat thdon (Lit. I ache in the area of the
intestines.)
'I have a stomach ache.!

b) ganuk means 'to be amusing, to be fun, to have a good
time, etc.! It may have a place, thing, person, or
activity as subject.

c) g@&j has the meaning 'get, obtain, procure! when used as
the main verb, as in the sentence ph&m d@j phfan maj
l%aj khon 'I made a lot of new friends.!
