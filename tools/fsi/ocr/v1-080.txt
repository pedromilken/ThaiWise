4,2 GRAMMAR DRILLS .
a) Response Drill
The instructor points at a student as he asks the question
and gives the actual name of the student as the cue. He
asks every student the question.
Questions: nén/nfi khraj khrap Who is that/this?
Cue: (actual name of student
pointed at)
Answers: nfi/n@n khun (Joe) This/that is (Joe).
khrap
b) Response Drill
The instructor gives a cue and points at a student.
One student asks and another answers questions like
the following:
Instructors khun th&awoon (pointing at a student)
Student 1: n&an/nfi khun th&awoon, That/this is Mr.
chaj maj khrap Thaworn, isn't it?
Student 2: maj chaj khrap No, it isn't.
Student 11 n&n/nfi khraj khrap Who is that/this?
Student 21 (nan/nfi) khun (That/this is)
1. khun cim 4.  khun coon
2. khruu 5. khruu khaw
3. khun meerfi 6. khun prasset
Repeat the drill using actual names of students in the class.
