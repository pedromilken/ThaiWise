21.6 EXERCISES
Using the format of the basic dialog as a guide plan an
excursion to some point of interest. Keep the following
points in mind:
1. Date and time of the trip,
2. The place,
3. The means of conveyance,
4. The persons included,
5. The arrangements for meeting beforehand,
6. What should be taken along, and
7. Any other relevant factors (weather conditions, etc.)
