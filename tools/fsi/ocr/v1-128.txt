7-2 GRAMMAR NOTES
a) kheej + Verb Phrase is used to refer to experience in
the past.
khaw kheej pen khruu 'He used to be a teacher.!
khun kheaj paj myanthaj maj 'Have you ever been to
Thailand?!
The affirmative response to kheaj + Verb Phrase + maj
questions is kheej; the negative response is maj khaej.
b) paj 'to got and maa 'to come! may be followed by place
expressions (roonrian, etc.) or by Verb Phrases indicat~
ing an activity Gn kh3on 'buy things!, etc.). Either
the place expression or Verb Phrase may be replaced by
n&j in questions, thus
Q: paj n&j Where are you going? (or) 'What are
you going (someplace) to do?!
As paj roonrian '(I'm) going to school.!
(or) paj syy khSon '(I'tm) going shopping.!
c) Since the verb in Thai does not have changes in form
to correlate with changes in time (tense changes), a
sentence like phim paj roonrian might be interpreted
as I'm going to school!, 'I go to school!, or 'I went
to school!. This does not usually result in ambiguity,
however, since the context the utterance occurs in
usually makes it clear which interpretation is intended.
