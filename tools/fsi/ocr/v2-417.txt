9. Students will find out from the instructor what the
Thais call sculptors, watch repairmen, hair dressers,
weavers, boat builders, house painters, carpenters,
printers, etc.

10. Student A asks Student B if he is going to some particular
place. When B indicates that he is, A asks him to do some
thing (buy something, etc.) for him, indicating that since
he is going there anyway it won't be any trouble for him
to do him a favor.

11. One student asks another what he plans to buy (as a present)
in Thailand for his mother, or his older sister, or his
material aunt, or some other relative.

12. Student A asks about the location of some object, or the
identity of some person in the classroom. Student B
indicates his surprise and points it out to him.

13. Student A plays the part of a bank teller and Student B
that of a customer. They discuss withdrawing and depositing
money in the bank.
