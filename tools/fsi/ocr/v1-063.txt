e) There is no distinction in form between the singular
and plural of nouns in Thai, thus to means ta table!
or 'tables' (in general).
However, plurality in general may be indicated by other
forms in the Noun Phrase, thuss
to did dii 'some good tables!
, where repeating the stative verb dii serves this purpose.
f) When jaa Jdontt! is repeated before a command or request
form, it is changed into a negative command or requests
peat pratuu khrap Open the door.
jaa peat pratuu khrap Don't open the door.
g) After verbs like phfut 'to say!, rfak 'to be called!, etc.
waa must be used.
phdm pha@ut waa jaa peat pratuu 'T said, "Don't open the
door.
