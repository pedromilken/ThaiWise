15.2 EXERCISES
One student will take the part of a Thai and will interview
another student who will take the part of an American who
has just arrived in a provincial city in Thailand for duty.
The interview should follow the general format of the one
in 15.0 but additional material may be included. As far as
possible, all information given should be correct. The other
students in the class should take notes on the interview and
be prepared to answer questions about the information contained
in it.
