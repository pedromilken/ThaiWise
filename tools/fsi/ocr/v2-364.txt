THAI BASIC COURSE
38.4 EXERCISES
a) Have one student take the part of a store clerk and
another that of a customer. Have them go through the
routine of purchasing various items, such as:
(a) a pack of cigarettes, (b) two bars of toilet soap,
(c) a tube of toothpaste, (d) some matches, etc.
The clerk asks the brand, kind, and size the customer
wants. When he is told, he indicates what the price
is. Then the customer inquires if there is anything
cheaper (better, etc.). Then he gives the clerk a bill
and asks for change.
