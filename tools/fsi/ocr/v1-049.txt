2.1 NOTES ON THE DIALOG

a) ch¥y means 'name! or 'to be named! but refers only to
the first or given name. :

b) naamsakun means 'family or last name!.

c) kh¥Sothdot means 'texcuse me! or tpardon met and is used
in the same way as the English words.

d) karunaa means 'please, kindly...! and is followed by a
request form.

e) iik thii 'again! means literally 'an additional time.!

f) khun is a polite title that may be used with names of
either sex. It is normally put before a person's given
name, since that is the name that one is usually addressed
by in Thailand.
khun aarii 'Miss Aree!, khun thaawoon 'Mr. Thaworn!,
khun noynkhraan 'Mrs. Nongkrant
Khun may be placed before the family names of foreigners,
thus khun braawn 'Mr. Brown!.
