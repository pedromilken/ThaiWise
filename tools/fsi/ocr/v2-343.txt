37.4 EXERCISES

a) Pretend you are buying material for making some
article of clothing. You take the part of the
buyer; another student, the seller.

b) Discuss the dimensions of various objects in the
room.

c) One student describes a certain object by giving its
dimensions and other characteristics (such as color,
price, use, etc.) of it. Another student tries to
guess what is being described.

d) Find out the weight and height of everyone in the
room, then make comparison such as 'John is 10 pounds
heavier than Mary', etc. (poon pound! may be used.)
