a) phdm, dich&n, khun, and kh3w are pronouns in Thai.
phdm means 'I!' and is used by males only.
dichan or dihan (or in formal usage dich%n) means 'I!

khun is a polite form for ‘youl.
khaw (in slow distinct speech kh3w) means the, she, or

1.4 EXERCISES

a) Each person inquires about the health of the person next
to him, to which that person replies that he is fine.

b) The instructor asks each student how he is, and each
student replies.

ec) Each student asks the instructor how he is, and the
instructor responds.

good. (The instructor should continue this exercise
