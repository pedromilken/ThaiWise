13.4 EXERCISES (Use a cardboard clock face with movable

hands with the following exercises.)

a) Set the hands on the clock face. One student asks
what time it is and another responds according to
the position of the hands.

b) Set the hands on the clock face, then ask different
students what time it will be in ten minutes, twenty
minutes, one hour, etc.

ec) One student asks another what time it is by his watch;
when he responds, the first student says that his watch
is slow or fast by a certain number of minutes according
to the time indicated by the classroom clock.
