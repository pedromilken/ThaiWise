a) In affirmative requests stative verbs (dii, chaa, etc.)
usually occur in reduplicated form when used as modifiers
of other verbs.
phout chaa chaa nodoj nakhrap 'Speak a little slower.!
ndoj ta little! usually occurs after the stative verb
and serves to soften the request.
In negative requests the stative verb is not usually
reduplicated.

nak 's0, so much! may occur after the stative verb.
