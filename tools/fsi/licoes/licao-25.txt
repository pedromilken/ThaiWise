25.3 EXERCISES

a) One student asks another what is the matter with him.
The second responds with some type of ailment.

b) One student asks another whether he considers certain
people, places, things, or activities to be amusing or
fun. The second responds according to his opinion. The
questions may be specific (the person you met at the
party last night, etc.) of they may be general (learning
Thai, etc.)

something (ex: Why did you go to bed late last night?).
Student B will give a reason (ext Because I went to a
