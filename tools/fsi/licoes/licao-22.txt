b) The verb jaak fwant to, would like! normally has a
sentence as a complement.
When the subject of the main verb (jaak) and of its
complement are the same, the subject of the comple-
ment does not occur.
As phdm jaak .... 1I would like ...1
B: phdm phop khun 1I meet you.!

A/Bt ph&m jaak phop khun 1I would like to meet you.!
When the subject of the main verb and of the complement
are different and the meaning is causative (i.e. 'to
have or cause someone to do something!) , both subjects
occur and h@j occurs after jaak.

At phdm jaak ... tI would like ...!
B: khaw phép khun 'He meets you.!
A/Bs ph&m jaak haj khaw phop khun 'I would like him to meet
you.!
